How to Scan a Business Card into Contacts on iPhone

Last updated 2026-10-22

Business cards tend to vanish into wallets right when you need to call someone back. When Paperkeep scans a card it files it as a business card and can open a new-contact screen pre-filled with what it read. You check the details and save it yourself, at no charge.

First, with iOS's built-in tools

  1. The basic route is the Contacts app: tap “+” and type the name and number while looking at the card.
  2. On supported iPhones, pointing the Camera at the card and touching and holding a phone number or email can offer to add it to a contact.
  3. Either way you handle one field at a time, and no image of the card is kept.

With Paperkeep

  1. Tap “Scan” and center the card in the frame. For a two-sided card, capture the back too, then tap “Save”.
  2. The “Photo” filter keeps logos and colors on the “New Scan” screen. Tap “Save” when it looks right.
  3. Open the card: its type shows as “Business card” and an “Add to Contacts” button appears. If Paperkeep filed it as something else, pick “Business card” under “Type” and the button shows up.
  4. Tap “Add to Contacts” to open a new contact pre-filled with the name, company, job title, phone, email and more. Correct any field, then tap “Done”.

Tips for this document

  • A white card on a white desk is hard to edge-detect; set it on something darker.
  • Cards with unusual layouts can put the title in the name field, so read each field before saving.
  • The scanned image stays in Paperkeep under “Smart Folders” → “Business Cards”, so a note you scribbled on the back is still there later.
