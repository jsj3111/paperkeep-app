FAQ

Questions

Where are my documents stored?

In the Files app › On My iPhone › Paperkeep, as ordinary PDFs. Open, rename, move or copy them there, and Paperkeep follows the changes the next time you open it.

Does Paperkeep upload my documents?

No. Paperkeep has no servers and no networking code. Text recognition and search run on your iPhone; only Apple's App Store handles purchases.

What's free?

Scanning, saving, organizing, search inside documents, sharing and printing as PDF, JPEG or PNG, saving business cards to Contacts, copying a page's text and App Lock. Free exports never get a watermark. Pro adds searchable PDFs, PDF tools like signing and redaction, ID card mode, receipt CSV, locked folders and file-name patterns.

Can I lock my documents?

Yes. Lock Paperkeep with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode — that's free. Locking individual folders is part of Pro. Locked documents are hidden from Spotlight, the widget and Shortcuts.

Text recognition got something wrong.

It reads on your iPhone and can make mistakes, especially on faded receipts. Rename the document or change its type from the document screen, and check important numbers.

What happens to my documents if I cancel Pro?

They stay. Cancelling never removes anything you've made, and searchable PDFs stay searchable. Cancel in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
