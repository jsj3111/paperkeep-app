Kami is licensed to you under Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the standard EULA). These pages add the plain-language details of Kami Pro.

Kami Pro

  • Yearly: includes a 14-day free trial for new subscribers, then renews every year.
  • Weekly: no free trial, renews every week.
  • Lifetime: one payment, not a subscription, no renewals.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase (for the yearly plan, when the free trial ends). Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If you start the trial, Kami can send one reminder 24 hours before you're charged.

Free features stay free

Scanning, saving, organizing, search, and sharing or printing PDF, JPEG and PNG are free, without watermarks. Documents you made with Pro stay yours and keep working if Pro ends.

Your documents

Your documents are stored only on your device and are your responsibility to back up. Text recognition and receipt totals are automatic and can be wrong; check important information.
