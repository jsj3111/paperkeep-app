Kami

The scanner that never uploads your documents.

Scan contracts, receipts, IDs and school forms into real PDFs that stay on your iPhone — in Files, protected by iOS, searchable inside Kami. No account. No upload. No watermark, ever.

  • Kami Pro: searchable PDFs, sign, mark up and redact, merge, split, compress, password, ID card mode
