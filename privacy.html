개인정보 처리방침 (한국어 요약)

Paperkeep은 데이터를 수집하지 않아요. 계정, 서버, 분석 도구, 외부 SDK가 없고 앱에 네트워크 코드가 없어요. 스캔한 문서, 인식한 글자, 제목·폴더·태그는 모두 이 iPhone 안(파일 앱 › 나의 iPhone › Paperkeep)에만 저장되고, iOS 데이터 보호로 잠금 중에는 읽을 수 없어요. 글자 인식과 모든 도구는 기기 안에서 실행돼요. 위젯과 Spotlight에는 제목과 종류만 보이고(본문은 절대 아님), 앱 잠금 중에는 아무것도 보이지 않아요. 결제는 Apple이 처리해요. 문의: 지원 페이지

전문 (영어 원문)

Paperkeep does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no third-party SDK in the app, and the app contains no networking code. Nothing you scan, type or do in Paperkeep is sent anywhere, and we have no way to see it.

Your documents stay on your iPhone

Scans, imported files, the text Paperkeep recognizes, titles, folders, tags and every edit are stored in Paperkeep's own storage on your iPhone. Your documents are ordinary PDF files in Files › On My iPhone › Paperkeep, protected by iOS data protection (unreadable while your iPhone is locked). If you use iCloud or device backups, iOS may include them in your own backup, under your Apple Account — never ours.

On-device processing

Edge detection, filters, text recognition (Apple's Vision framework), Live Text selection, document types, receipt totals and every PDF tool run on your iPhone. Visual Look Up, which would contact Apple's servers, is turned off.

What leaves the app's screens — still on your iPhone

  • Widgets show the titles of your most recent documents (never page images or text), and nothing while App Lock is on.
  • Spotlight search can find documents by title and type (never their text); locked documents are not indexed.
  • Shortcuts can open the scanner or a document by its title.
  • Your signature, if you draw one, is kept in this iPhone's Keychain only (not synced).

Permissions

Camera: only to scan. Contacts: only when you tap "Add to Contacts" on a business card, and Apple's contact sheet lets you review before saving. Face ID: only if you turn on a lock. Photos: Paperkeep uses the system photo picker, which gives it only the photos you pick.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Paperkeep sees whether Pro is active on this device; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Paperkeep collects no data from anyone, including children.
